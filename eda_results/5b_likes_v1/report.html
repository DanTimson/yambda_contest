Yambda exploratory analysis

Local data only. No model training. All charts and tables embedded for offline reading.

Purpose and boundaries

Explore the next 100 likes per requested user before choosing recommendation models. Primary targets are the next 100 chronological like events. Distinct-future-item and first-ever-item variants quantify how much deduplication and past-like exclusion matter. There is no fixed 30-day target window. The public log end censors future observations. This report is descriptive; inspecting a period makes it development data.

What to decide from these results

Check requested-user coverage before choosing data scale; choose historical cutoffs with enough observed future; compare repeat-like target variants before hard exclusions; inspect popularity churn before picking recency scales; measure familiar-artist and prior-listen target support before investing in candidate sources. Popularity diagnostics are references, not a reason to fit CatBoost yet. Keep a later period uninspected for final model auditing.
